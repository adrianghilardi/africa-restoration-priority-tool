# Change log

## 1.0.0

First versioned landscape-screening release for Congo Basin and KAZA.

- MoFuSS annual-frame accounting for pressure and conditional recovery, with separately documented 2020–2050 and 2030–2050 windows.
- Run-specific Chapman–Richards recovery and deterministic growth-rate/threshold sensitivities.
- Exact fractional-cell province, ecoregion and TNC planning-zone summaries; landscape-specific four-class pressure typology with explicit coverage requirements.
- Observed-biomass-change context and separate model-mass versus geodesic-area accounting.
- Reproducible CCKP climate downloads and native-resolution scenario/spread/significance products.
- SSP population pathways and observed national poverty context, crossed with constant/half-rate sensitivities and explicit geographic coverage checks.
- Portable data/configuration, input checksums, tests, numerical validation, sample maps, analyst guide and data dictionary.

See the release acceptance report for evidence from the exact shipped version. The limitations in `V1_ASSUMPTIONS_AND_V2.md` are part of the interpretation contract for this screening release, not hidden defaults.
